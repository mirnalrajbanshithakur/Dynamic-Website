About

Defence is a habit, not a product.

I work in IT security with a simple view: most incidents start with something ordinary, such as a weak password, an unpatched service or a convincing email. Good security closes those gaps quietly and makes the rest visible.

My work covers securing networks and endpoints, reading logs to spot unusual activity, testing systems for weaknesses and helping people understand the risks they face in plain language.

I am 22 and a curious learner who enjoys seeing how computers and software solve real problems. I am comfortable with C, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I keep improving through practice and projects.
